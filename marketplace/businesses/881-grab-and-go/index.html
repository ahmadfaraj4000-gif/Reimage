Services and specialties

What 881 Grab & Go offers.

881 Grab & Go combines island comfort food with Philly attitude in Hartford. The menu spans oxtail, jerk chicken, vegan curry chickpeas, and the signature Cheyney Fish Sandwich: fried whiting layered with 881 aioli, pickled cabbage, and cheese.
