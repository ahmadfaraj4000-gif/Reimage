Services and specialties

What Fish N Tingz Bar & Grill offers.

Fish N Tingz brings West Indian food and drinks to 384 Middle Turnpike W in Manchester. Order oxtail tacos, whole snapper, or jerk chicken, meet friends over a Rum Punch Tower, or bring the family for Sunday brunch. Request a table online; the restaurant will contact you to confirm availability and arrangements.
