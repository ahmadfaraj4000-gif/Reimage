Services and specialties

What Amanah Medical Care offers.

Amanah Medical Care connects physician expertise with healthcare teams in resource-limited settings. Rooted in Islamic values of service and compassion, the initiative is developing a model for consultation, medical education, and hospital collaboration. Its website provides information about physician participation and institutional partnerships.
