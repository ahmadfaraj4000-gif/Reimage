Services and specialties

What Living Word Imprints offers.

Living Word Imprints has served the Hartford area since 1995 as a one-stop print shop. Services include screen printing, direct-to-garment printing, embroidery, custom apparel, school uniforms, signs, banners, business cards, brochures, labels, promotional products, and general copying and printing, with Hartford and East Hartford locations.
