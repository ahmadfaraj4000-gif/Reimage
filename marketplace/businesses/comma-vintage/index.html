Comma Vintage

Comma Vintage finds rare vintage menswear and packs subscription boxes by hand, choosing pieces to suit each subscriber’s size and style.

Services and specialties

What Comma Vintage offers.

One box might hold a single-stitch tee from the ’80s. Another might pair American-made denim with a vintage accessory. Comma Vintage uses a short survey about fit and personal style to choose pieces for each subscriber, then includes a note about the finds inside.

Quick answers

Know before you go.

How does Comma Vintage choose clothes for my box?+

After you subscribe, you fill out a survey about your size, fit, and style. The team uses your answers to pick vintage pieces by hand.

What comes in a Comma Vintage box?+

Each box has a different mix of vintage menswear, accessories, and a personal note about the pieces. The Comma Vintage website has examples of past boxes.
