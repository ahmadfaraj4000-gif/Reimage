Services and specialties

What Fusion Health Juice Bar offers.

Fusion Health Juice Bar brings fresh, energizing flavor to Hartford with Jamaican-inspired juices, smoothies, fruit bowls, wellness shots, and sea moss drinks. Its signature Peanut Punch blends peanut, banana, nutmeg, vanilla, oats, protein, almond milk, and honey for a rich local favorite.
