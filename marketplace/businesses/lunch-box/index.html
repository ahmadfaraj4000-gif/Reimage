What Lunch Box offers.

Lunch Box serves Hartford with Caribbean takeout and catering made for everyday meals and larger gatherings. Favorites include Jerk Chicken, Curry Chicken, Escovitch Fish, customizable lunch boxes, callaloo, fried dumplings, natural drinks, and full party trays.
