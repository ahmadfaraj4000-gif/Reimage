Services and specialties

What Blue Hills Cafe offers.

Blue Hills Cafe brings Caribbean flavor and Hartford hospitality to 1329 Albany Avenue. Meet friends at the bar, plan a birthday or private celebration, or ask about Jamaican catering from Caribe Exquisite Catering. The cafe also offers $5 Thursday meals and bottle-service inquiries. Contact the team for current hours, this week’s dishes, event availability, guest capacity, and pricing.

Quick answers

Know before you go.

Can I host a private event at Blue Hills Cafe?+

Yes. Blue Hills welcomes inquiries for birthdays, private parties, bridal showers, baby showers, and other gatherings. Contact the team to confirm availability, guest capacity, and rental rates.

What is the Thursday special?+

Blue Hills Cafe offers $5 meals every Thursday. Call 860-436-1553 for this week’s dishes and serving times.

Does Blue Hills Cafe offer catering?+

Caribbean and Jamaican catering is available through Caribe Exquisite Catering. Email caribeexquisitecatering@gmail.com to discuss your occasion and menu.

What are the opening hours?+

Call 860-436-1553 for current opening hours.
