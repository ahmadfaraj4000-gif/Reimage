Our process

What gets checked.

Before a listing is published, we check the business name, official website, physical address or truthful service area, direct customer action, core specialties, and image attribution. Each profile shows the date its information was last verified.

Corrections and updates

Business owners can request a correction through RE IMAGE. We update factual details when a current official source or owner confirmation supports the change.

Directory information last reviewed September 4, 2026.

What you will not find

No invented trust signals.

We do not publish fabricated ratings, copied reviews, unsupported “best” claims, or fake storefront addresses. Paid placements are labeled Sponsored; editorial selections are labeled RE IMAGE Pick.

How listings are organized

Hartford storefronts appear first, followed by nearby Greater Hartford businesses, other Connecticut services, and a separate section for businesses beyond Greater Hartford.

Keep it accurate

Own or manage a listed business?
