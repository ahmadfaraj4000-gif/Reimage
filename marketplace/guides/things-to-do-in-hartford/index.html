Things to do in Hartford, CT

Spend a first afternoon downtown, make lunch the reason for a neighborhood visit, or pick a spot for a night out. Choose a plan below and check the venue for your date.

  • A neighborhood lunch

A downtown afternoon

Start at the Wadsworth Atheneum Museum of Art at 600 Main Street. The galleries make a good rainy-day plan. If the weather clears, head to Mortensen Riverfront Plaza at 300 Columbus Boulevard for a view of the Connecticut River. Hartford residents can check the museum’s resident admission program before buying a ticket.

  • Museum hours and admission
  • Hartford resident admission

Two stops for lunch on one block

At 879 New Britain Avenue, Fusion Health Juice Bar serves Peanut Punch and fresh juices. Next door at 881 New Britain Avenue, 881 Grab & Go serves a fried-whiting sandwich with pickled cabbage. For jerk chicken or escovitch fish on a different outing, head to Lunch Box at 104 Baltimore Street.

  • Explore the New Britain Avenue block
  • Lunch Box menu and ordering

Mini golf, cocktails and live music

For a date night with something to do between drinks, try the putting green at The Patio, 2280 Main Street. Check the venue’s calendar if you want to catch a DJ or a live performance. For a Jamaican dinner instead, Jam Roc Restaurant & Lounge serves jerk chicken and seafood at 3395 Main Street.

  • The Patio’s menu and events
  • Jam Roc dining details

What’s happening this weekend?

Riverfront Recapture’s calendar lists events by date and park. Open an event to check its start time, meeting point and registration details. Look at the town as well as the park name: the calendar includes both Hartford and East Hartford. If you’re coming from out of town and need a car, Rent Me CT lists vehicles in nearby Farmington.
