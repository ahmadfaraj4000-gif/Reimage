RE IMAGE Terminal privacy notice

Effective October 16, 2026

RE IMAGE Terminal helps authorized business staff connect a business website to a payment reader. This notice describes information processed by the Terminal app and its supporting service.

Information used to provide the service

We process employee email addresses and account identifiers, business and location assignments, device names and identifiers, reader serial numbers, order references and totals, payment identifiers and status, and operational records such as request timestamps and errors. These records support sign-in, authorization, payment processing, troubleshooting, and fraud prevention.

With your permission, Bluetooth discovers and connects to payment readers. Location permission supports Stripe Terminal's payment-location requirements. Stripe may process device and location information through its Terminal SDK. The app's backend does not store raw card numbers or card security codes.

Service providers and business access

Amazon Web Services hosts authentication, operational records and backend services. Stripe processes payment information and provides reader functionality. Your business's authorized administrators can manage staff and devices and review that business's payment records. A website opened inside Terminal may have its own privacy notice and data practices.

Storage and retention

The mobile app stores sign-in tokens and device credentials in protected operating-system storage. Payment and audit records are retained to operate the service, investigate errors, reconcile transactions, and meet applicable business requirements. Operational server logs are configured for 30-day retention. Stripe and your business may retain their own records under their policies.

Your choices

You can revoke Bluetooth or location permission in device settings, though reader functionality may then be unavailable. Ask your business administrator to revoke staff access or device approval. Contact RE IMAGE to request access to, correction of, or deletion of account information; some payment records may need to be retained for reconciliation or other applicable requirements.

Advertising and tracking

RE IMAGE Terminal does not include advertising SDKs or sell personal information. Its native app does not use cross-app advertising tracking. Third-party websites opened in the app remain responsible for their own content and data collection.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: reimagebs@gmail.com. Do not include payment card details, passwords, or secret keys.
