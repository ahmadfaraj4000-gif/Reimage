Your website. Connected to payments.

RE IMAGE Terminal connects an approved business website to its assigned Stripe Reader M2 through the iPhone or iPad app. Access is provided by your business administrator.

Testing your connection

Open RE IMAGE Terminal, sign in, select your business and register your device. After your administrator approves it, turn on Use Stripe simulated reader (test only), find and connect the simulated reader, then open the business checkout.

The RE IMAGE test checkout includes Foundation ($999), Business Hub ($1,899), and Operations ($2,499) sandbox setup orders. Enterprise is custom quoted and cannot be charged without an approved quote. No monthly service is charged in this test. Sandbox payments do not charge real cards. A physical M2 is required for real card-present transactions.

Recovering an interrupted payment

If a payment is interrupted, use Check payment status before trying again. Keep the existing order open until its status is known. Never assume that a disconnected reader means the payment failed.

Get help

Contact reimagebs@gmail.com for setup, device approval, account access, or support. Include your business name and payment request ID when relevant. Do not send card numbers, passwords, or API keys.
